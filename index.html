Who Am I

I’ve been fully immersed in the crypto space since 2017. Over the years, I’ve seen the industry evolve through every phase — from the early ICO boom to the rise of DeFi, the explosion of NFTs, meme-coin mania, yield farming, and liquidity providing. I’ve tested, tried, failed, succeeded, and learned more than I can count along the way.

After spending years navigating the good, the bad, and everything in between, I decided to create Bullsonchain. My goal is simple: to share the knowledge and insights I’ve gathered, help others avoid the mistakes I made, and keep you updated with the most important news and trends that actually matter in today’s crypto market.

Whether you’re new to the space or already deep in it, Bullonchain is here to help you stay informed, think smarter, and move confidently in the ever-changing world of crypto.
